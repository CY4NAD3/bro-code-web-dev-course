Dragon:
A dragon is a large, serpentine or reptilian mythical creature found in the folklore, art, and traditions of cultures all over the world.
Phoenix:
: A legendary immortal bird from Greek, Egyptian, and Persian mythology that cyclically regenerates or is reborn from the ashes of its predecessor.
Vampire:
A vampire is a mythical, undead humanoid creature that subsists by feeding on the blood or vital essence of the living.
Werewolf:
A werewolf (from the Old English werwulf, meaning "man-wolf") is a mythological or fictional human who shapeshifts into a wolf or a hybrid wolf-human creature.
